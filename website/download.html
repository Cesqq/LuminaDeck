After install

  1. Launch LuminaDeck Studio. It sits in your system tray.
  2. On your iPhone, install LuminaDeck from the App Store.
  3. Open the phone app, tap Connect, and scan the QR code Studio shows.
  4. That's it. Tap tiles on your phone, fire macros on your PC.

System requirements

  • Windows 10 version 2004 or later / Windows 11 (x64)
  • ~40 MB disk, ~60 MB RAM while running
  • Local WiFi network shared with your iPhone
  • Firewall inbound on ports 9876 and 9877 for authenticated local pairing/control
