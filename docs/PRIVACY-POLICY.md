# LuminaDeck Privacy Policy

**Effective Date**: April 2026
**Last Updated**: April 2026

## Overview

LuminaDeck ("the App") is a macro deck application that lets you control your Windows PC from your phone (iPhone or Android) over your local Wi-Fi network. The Windows companion is also distributed via the Microsoft Store. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

## Data We Collect

### Data We Do NOT Collect
- We do not collect analytics or usage telemetry unless you explicitly enable optional diagnostics
- We do not track your location
- We do not share any data with third parties
- We do not use advertising SDKs
- We do not record keystrokes or button presses
- We do not access your contacts, messages, or other personal data

### Data Stored Locally On Your Device
The following data is stored only on your phone and your PC, never transmitted to our servers:

- **Button layouts and profiles**: Your custom button configurations are saved to your device's local storage
- **Connection history**: IP addresses of paired PCs and QR pairing secrets are stored in your device's secure keychain
- **Settings preferences**: Theme, haptic feedback, and grid layout preferences
- **Pairing material**: The companion stores hashed pairing secrets so only paired devices can send commands

### Data We May Collect (Optional)
If you choose to create an account for Pro license management:

- **Email address**: Used only for account authentication
- **Purchase receipt**: Apple In-App Purchase transaction ID, validated for license entitlement

This data is stored in our Supabase backend and is not shared with any third parties.

## Network Communication

- All communication between your phone and PC occurs over your **local Wi-Fi network only**
- Core PC control happens on your local Wi-Fi network and requires QR-paired device authentication
- No data is routed through our servers or any cloud relay
- mDNS discovery is used only during device pairing and is stopped once pairing completes

## Third-Party Services

- **Apple In-App Purchase**: Used for Pro upgrade processing on iOS. Subject to Apple's privacy policy.
- **Google Play Billing**: Used for Pro upgrade processing on Android. Subject to Google's privacy policy.
- **Microsoft Store**: Used for distribution and licensing of the Windows companion. Subject to Microsoft's privacy policy.
- **RevenueCat**: Used for purchase receipt validation across iOS and Android. RevenueCat processes only your anonymous App Store / Play Store transaction data. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.
- **Supabase**: Used for optional account management. See Supabase's privacy policy at https://supabase.com/privacy.

## Children's Privacy

LuminaDeck does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The App is rated 4+ and contains no objectionable content.

## Data Retention

- Local data is retained until you delete it or uninstall the App
- Account data (if created) is retained until you request deletion
- Purchase history is maintained by Apple, Google, or Microsoft (depending on platform) per their retention policies

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- **Access**: View all data stored by the App (visible in Settings)
- **Delete**: Remove all local data by uninstalling the App
- **Account deletion**: Request deletion of any account data by contacting us
- **Export**: Export your button profiles as JSON files

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on our website and reflected in the "Last Updated" date above.
